# Trail 13/14 route sources

Authentic organizer or participant GPX remains the preferred source and should be archived here when secured.

The active 2026 working route is now materialized at:

`routes/ost/trail14-current-reference/route.gpx`

It is **not** an organizer/participant GPX. It is the documented 13.472 km provisional Hallamölla splice derived from the verified Trail 21/22 2022 public map geometry. It removes the 8.269 km eastern loop between two route points only 0.18 m apart, leaving Hallamölla at approximately 8.687 km and 4.785 km to the finish. The hypothesis is independently supported by organizer raster topology and a verified 2023 participant activity (13.67 km; about 4.5 km remaining at Hallamölla).

The frontend must label it **Rekonstruerad bana · provisorisk**. Historical 13/14 km years remain unassigned until year-specific geometry is verified.
