# Christinehof corridor controls

Christinehofs Ekopark publishes an official hiking network from the castle parking area.

Named routes:
- Borstakärr 1.4 km: Christinehof – Trädgårdsmästarbostaden – Södra Borstakärr – Gamla allén – Christinehof.
- Borstakärr 2.7 km: Christinehof – Trädgårdsmästarbostaden – Södra Borstakärr – Norra Borstakärr – Christinehof.
- Promenaden 3.6 km: Christinehof – Trädgårdsmästarbostaden – Södra Borstakärr – Skogsvägen – Christinehof.
- Hallamöllaleden 8.5 km: Christinehof – Alunbruket – Verkeån south via Skåneleden – Hallamölla – Verkafuret – Verkasjön – Alunbruket – Christinehof.
- Fiskabäcksleden 12 km extends the Verkeån/Skåneleden corridor via Fiskabäck.

## Trail 13/14

Three independent evidence types now agree on Hallamölla as part of the race corridor:

1. The current ÖST 14 km Race-PM says the course is one loop with its midpoint at Hallamölla.
2. The official Ekopark trail network connects Christinehof to Hallamölla through the Alunbruket / Verkeån / Skåneleden corridor.
3. The verified 2023 participant narrative says Hallamölla was reached with about 4.5 km remaining.

This materially narrows the eastern route reconstruction, but does not by itself prove every race junction.

## Trail 5

The georeferenced organizer raster remains route-choice authority and its manually sampled centreline measured 5.002 km. The official Borstakärr and Promenaden routes are useful mapped-corridor controls near Christinehof, but none should be substituted for the race course merely because it is an official hiking route.

## Rule

Use:
1. organizer race raster for race-specific branch choice;
2. mapped path geometry for exact centreline;
3. official Ekopark named corridors for local network validation;
4. participant race-day GPS/narrative for independent validation;
5. heatmaps and satellite imagery only as supporting QA.

Do not invent OSM way IDs or a snapped GPX before raw vector geometry has been acquired and inspected.
