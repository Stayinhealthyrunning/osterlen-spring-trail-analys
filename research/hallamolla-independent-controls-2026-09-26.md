# Hallamölla independent geometry controls — 2026-09-26

Additional controls found while reconstructing the Trail 13/14 eastern loop:

- Länsstyrelsen Skåne publishes a mapped Hallamöllaleden as a red dotted route and distinguishes it from Skåneleden, Backaleden and Fiskabäcksleden.
- Tomelilla municipality states that the Hallamöllaleden segment from Alunbruket's eastern parking area to Hallamölla is approximately 2.5 km, mostly easy trail, marked red.
- A public Wikiloc activity, trail id 52431996, contains 1,700 recorded coordinates and explicitly references Hallamöllaleden; its downloadable file requires login. It is therefore a validation lead, not imported geometry.
- The organizer's current 14 km PM states one lap, Hallamölla as midpoint, and warns about narrow paths with steep sides along Verkeån.

These controls strengthen the eastern corridor independently of the race raster. They do not prove every junction choice.
