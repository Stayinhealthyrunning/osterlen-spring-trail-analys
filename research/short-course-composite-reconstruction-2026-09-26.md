# Short-course composite reconstruction — 2026-09-26

## Finding

The organizer's legacy 13 km raster is not an isolated unknown loop. It can be decomposed into two recognizable corridor systems:

1. an eastern Christinehof–Alunbruket–Verkeån–Hallamölla–Verkasjön/Verkafuret–Alunbruket–Christinehof loop; and
2. a western Christinehof loop that overlaps the organizer's separate 5 km raster.

The official Christinehof trail network independently defines Hallamöllaleden as 8.5 km with the sequence:

Christinehof → Alunbruket → Verkeån south via Skåneleden → Hallamölla → Verkafuret → Verkasjön → Alunbruket → Christinehof.

The current race PM independently states that the approximately 14 km race is one lap with Hallamölla as midpoint.

## Reconstruction consequence

Treat Hallamöllaleden as the primary named-corridor skeleton for the eastern portion, then constrain its exact race usage with the organizer raster. Treat the organizer 5 km raster as a second registration of the western Christinehof corridor.

This is stronger than matching the 13/14 km raster against an unconstrained path network because both halves have independent controls.

## Distance sanity check

Official Hallamöllaleden: 8.5 km.
Organizer Naturlopp: approximately 5 km.
Simple sum: approximately 13.5 km.

This is compatible with both the historical 13 km+ label, the current approximately 14 km label, and the 2023 participant-reported 13.67 km race segment.

Distance agreement is supporting evidence only. It must not be used to assert that the race is exactly Hallamöllaleden + exactly the 5 km course.
